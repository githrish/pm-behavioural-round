# Step 2 — Mine & rank stories

## 2a. Mine everything

Go through the resume line by line. Every project, launch, initiative, deal, campaign, migration, process change or award is a candidate. Also ask the user for **off-resume stories**, which resumes hide but behavioral rounds love:
- a failure or a launch that flopped
- a conflict with a peer, manager or another team
- pushing back on a senior person
- a decision made with incomplete data
- a time they changed their mind because of users or data

Ask for these **one at a time** (failure first, then conflict, then pushing back, then incomplete data). Accept a one-line answer, or "none", for each.

## 2b. Score

Score each candidate 1–5:

| Criterion | 5 looks like | 1 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Company-level metric, large revenue/user base, exec visibility, many stakeholders | Small task, one person affected |
| **Complexity** | Many teams, conflicting goals, ambiguity, tech or timeline pressure, things went wrong | Straightforward execution |
| **Recency** | Last 2 years | 5+ years ago |
| **PM signal** | Clearly shows a PM competency below, with the user's own judgment visible | Pure execution of someone else's plan |

PM competencies to tag (1–3 per story):
- **Product sense / customer insight:** found a real user problem, discovery, research, insight that changed direction
- **Execution / delivery:** shipped something hard, managed scope, trade-offs, timelines
- **Data & metrics:** defined success metrics, experiments, analysis that drove a decision
- **Prioritisation & strategy:** chose what *not* to do, made bets, built a roadmap or a business case
- **Influence & stakeholders:** aligned people without authority, handled conflict or pushback
- **Leadership & ownership:** took charge of something unowned, grew people, raised the bar
- **Technical fluency:** worked closely with engineering on architecture or feasibility trade-offs
- **Failure & learning:** something went wrong, they owned it and changed how they work

For people moving into PM, score **PM signal** after applying the translation lens in `03-pm-translation.md`. A sales deal can score high if it involved discovery and shaping the product.

## 2c. Recommend, then let the user pick

Show a ranked table of the top 5–6:

| Rank | Story (working title) | When | Scope | Complexity | Recency | PM signal | Total | Competencies |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Under it, recommend a **top 3** that together cover **different** competencies, ideally:
1. a big **execution/impact** story (the one they'd most want to be asked about)
2. an **influence/conflict** story
3. a **product sense or data-driven decision** story

A **failure** story is very useful too. If it isn't among the top 3, it becomes a backup story in step 6.

Explain the choice in 2–3 lines, then ask: "Which 3 do you want to go deep on? You can swap any of mine." ⏸ Wait for their answer.

**Founder, side-project and current-venture stories count.** If the user runs or ran their own product (a startup, a side project with real users, a freelance product), list each of its achievements as separate candidates and score them like any job. They are usually the most recent stories, show full ownership, and also feed the "why are you leaving / why go back to a job" answer.
