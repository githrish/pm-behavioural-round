# Step 2: Intake (at most 3 questions before researching)

The research is only as useful as its aim. Three things change what you look for: the role, who the user is meeting, and what they personally need to find out. Ask only what the user hasn't already told you. One question per call of the question tool.

## Before asking anything

Read whatever the user gave you: the JD (text, file or link), the company's careers page if the JD is a link, and `story-bank.md` if it exists. From the JD, note:
- the team or product area the role sits in (e.g. "Payments, merchant onboarding")
- the level (APM, PM, Senior PM, Group PM)
- the 5 to 10 keywords and phrases the JD repeats (these go in the .md for Skill 3 to mirror)
- anything it reveals about the company's priorities ("0 to 1", "scale", "monetisation", "B2B expansion")

If the company name is ambiguous (two companies with the same name, or a parent and a brand), settle that first by searching. Only ask if you really can't tell.

## The questions (in this order, skipping any already answered)

**Q1. The role and JD** (skip if both were given)
"Which role is this for? Paste the JD or a link if you have one; your own words are welcome."
Options: infer from what you know, e.g. "PM, I'll paste the JD", "Just the title: Product Manager", "No JD yet, general PM role".

**Q2. Who you're meeting** (skip if the user already said)
"Who is the first round with? Add names if you know them, and I'll look up their work background to sharpen the questions."
Options: "Recruiter or talent screen", "Hiring manager", "A PM peer", "Founder or CEO". If they give names, run track F (work-related only; see `02-research-tracks.md`).

**Q3. Your signals: Lenny's question** (always ask)
"What do you need to find out to decide whether you want this job? Pick any, or tell me in your own words."
Use multi-select. Offer 4 options inferred from the company type, for example:
- Early startup: "How long their money lasts", "How much I'd own", "How the founders make decisions", "Where the product goes next"
- Growth-stage: "Whether growth is healthy or bought", "Team and manager style", "Scope and growth path", "Path to profitability"
- Large or listed company: "Real scope of the role", "How PM works with eng and design", "How decisions get made", "Why the role is open"

Record the answers word for word in the .md under "Your Signals". Every question to ask the interviewer must serve at least one of them.

## After the intake

React in one line ("Got it, so runway and ownership are the two that matter. I'll dig hardest there.") and launch the research. Don't ask anything else before the subagents start.
